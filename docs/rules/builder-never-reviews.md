# The builder never reviews their own work

Whoever built a change is never one of its reviewers. Reviewers get the change, the brief and the project's rules, not the builder's reasoning.

**Why:** A reviewer who reads the builder's reasoning tends to agree with it, and repeats the same mistake. A fresh reader catches the misunderstanding instead.

**How to apply:**

- Pick reviewers with the `review-panel` skill.
- Reviewers read the report's Assumptions only after their own first pass.
- For high-risk changes, add a second, different model as an independent reviewer.

**Where it lives:** the `review-panel` skill; every role file.

Origin: field-tested.

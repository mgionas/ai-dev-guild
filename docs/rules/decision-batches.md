# Ask the owner in batches, with recommendations

Collect every open question into one file, with a recommendation and a reason for each, and ask once: "Accept all" or "I'll change some".

**Why:** A chain of single questions stalls the work. With recommendations to accept, an owner can answer a batch of dozens in minutes.

**How to apply:**

- Questions for a lawyer, an accountant or users are open facts, not questions for the owner.
- Record the answers in the decision log the same turn, quoting the owner.

**Where it lives:** the `decision-batch` skill.

Origin: field-tested.

# Research section for a brief

Add this to the brief when the task is to find facts rather than change code.

## Sources

- Give every number you report the URL it came from. A number with no URL is not reported as a
  finding.
- When you cannot find a figure, write "not found". Never estimate it to fill the gap.

## Result

Label every figure in the result with one of three words:

- **Sourced:** it comes from a page you read, and the URL is beside it.
- **Assumption:** you chose it, and the line says why and what it rests on.
- **Gap:** you looked and found nothing. List what you searched.

A reader should be able to count the gaps without reading the whole result.

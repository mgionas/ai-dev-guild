---
name: decision-batch
description: Collect every open question for the product owner into one file with a recommendation per item, then ask once with an "Accept all" option. Use whenever product, money, legal or naming questions pile up.
---

People answer fastest when they get every question at once, each with a recommendation they can
accept. A long chain of single questions stalls the work.

1. **Collect** every open question: from the question sweep, review findings, builders'
   Questions, and assumptions that were really product choices.
2. **De-duplicate** and order them, most decision-relevant first.
3. **Write one file**, `.work/decisions/<date>-<topic>.md`, from `batch-template.md`: for each
   item, the question, your recommendation, and why in one or two sentences. Where two reviewers
   disagree, show both and give one combined recommendation.
4. **Send the file and ask once**, with a single question: "Accept all (recommended)" or "I'll
   change some". If the owner changes some, ask only about those.
5. **Record the answers the same turn** in the project's decision log, quoting the owner.

Questions for a lawyer, an accountant or users are not asked to the owner as if they knew. List
them as open facts with who must answer.
